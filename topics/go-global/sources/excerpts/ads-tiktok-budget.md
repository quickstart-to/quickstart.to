# About budgets for ads in TikTok Ads Manager

- URL: https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/budget?lang=en
- Accessed: 2026-10-10
- 定位：Minimum Budget Guidelines；Daily Budget vs. Lifetime Budget

> Ad group level: Budget must exceed $20

支持范围：2026-07 更新文档列每日系列预算超过 50 USD、广告组超过 20 USD；广告组总预算最低按天数计算；总预算非每日均分，上线后不能切换预算类型。
