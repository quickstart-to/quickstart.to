# Eligibility requirements for AdSense

来源：https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en

读取：2026-10-10，Ego TaskSpace 82。

> Your content must be high-quality, original, and attract an audience.

定位：Do you have your own unique and interesting content? / Does your content comply… / Are you at least 18 years old?；资格页还要求能访问 HTML source code。只采用这些条件，不推断审核时长。

采用范围：官方要求原创高质量内容、符合政策、能够访问提交站点 HTML，申请者需达到年龄条件；资格要求不等于个案通过承诺。
