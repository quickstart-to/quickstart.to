# Domain Review

- Source: https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification/what-is-domain-verification
- Publisher: Paddle
- Accessed: 2026-10-08

> If your submission requires a manual review by our team, this is typically completed within an estimated 5-7 business days.
> 
> Be aware that you will only be allowed to sell through the domain(s) that have been approved.

> Terms and Conditions, Refund Policy, and Privacy Policy (these must be clearly accessible via navigation on your website)

> Site must be live and secured with an SSL certificate (HTTPS)

> This does not apply to the Sandbox environment, which can be used without domain approval for testing.

本轮补核：网站还需清楚展示产品、价格及交付内容。已通过主域名审核不等于子域名自动获批；部分产品会要求测试账户或权利证明。
