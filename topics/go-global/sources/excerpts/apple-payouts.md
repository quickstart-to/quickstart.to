# Overview of receiving payments

- 来源：https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments/
- 发布方：Apple Developer
- 核验日期：2026-10-09
- 结论：收款需有效付费App协议、银行资料、达到地区门槛及适用开票要求；按Apple财务月结算，可能有银行费
- 置信度：confirmed（官方公开文档；不是个案审核或实测）

> Have a Paid Apps Agreement in effect.
