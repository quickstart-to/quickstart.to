# When and how do I get paid?

- Source: https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/when-and-how-do-i-get-paid
- Publisher: Paddle
- Accessed: 2026-10-09

> 1st of the month: As long as your account balance is over the payout threshold you set in your settings (min $100), your balance will convert to a payout ...
> 2nd-15th of the month: Paddle will send your payment by the 15th.
> 
> You can receive your payment either via wire transfer or Payoneer.

## 2026-10-09 提现章复核

定位：1st of the month / 2nd–15th / Threshold for payouts。每月处理，不支持随时提现；币种对应最低 100 USD/GBP/EUR。sent 状态说明资金在处理末段，实际发送后邮件给发票，官方估计依接收方式最多再需 3 个工作日。
