# About spending limits

- URL: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637?hl=en
- Accessed: 2026-10-10
- 定位：Daily spending limit；Monthly spending limit；served costs vs billed costs

> For most campaigns, the daily spending limit is your average daily budget multiplied by 2.

支持范围：多数日预算系列的单日可计费上限为平均日预算两倍，整月通常为 30.4 倍；预算变动/开始日期和按转化付费例外另算，served 与 billed cost 不同。
