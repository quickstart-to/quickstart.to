# Account Reviews

- Source: https://docs.creem.io/merchant-of-record/account-reviews/account-reviews
- Publisher: Creem
- Accessed: 2026-10-08

> Before you can accept live payments through Creem, your store needs to pass an account review.

> Account reviews are typically completed within 1-2 working days. During peak periods, it may take up to 3 working days.

> AI image/video generation products must integrate the CREEM Moderation API.

> All restricted products require additional verification, including previous payment processor details, chargeback/refund rate, and reason for moving to Creem. An established and proven track record is required for consideration.

## 2026-10-09 样章复核定位

定位：Approval checklist（产品可用、真实信息、价格、法律页面、支持邮箱）；Review process（通常1–2工作日，高峰3日；Balance → Payout Account）；Common reasons（邮箱不一致、无法访问、未完成等）；What information is needed（零元审核试购）。本文只把这些作为官方流程，不将个案一晚通过写成通用时效。
