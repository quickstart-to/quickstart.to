# About advanced location options

- URL: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en
- Accessed: 2026-10-10
- 定位：Compare location targeting options；Presence；Keep in mind

> Location targeting is based on a variety of signals, including users’ settings, devices, and behavior on our platform.

支持范围：默认 Presence or Interest 包括对地区有兴趣的人；Presence 限可能位于或经常位于目标地区的人；定位不能保证百分百准确。
