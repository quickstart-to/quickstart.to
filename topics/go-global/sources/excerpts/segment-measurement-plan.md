URL: https://www.twilio.com/docs/segment/protocols/tracking-plan/best-practices
Read: 2026-10-10
Locator: Define your Track events

> Segment recommends starting with fewer events that are directly tied to one of your business objectives.

Scope: 建议从业务目标定义少量核心事件，保持事件与属性命名稳定；也说明先多采集再分析有不同利弊
