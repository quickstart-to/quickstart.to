# Instructions for Form 5472 (12/2024)

- Source: https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472
- Publisher: Internal Revenue Service
- Accessed: 2026-10-09

> Generally, a reporting corporation must file Form 5472 if it had a reportable transaction with a foreign or domestic related party.

同时核对Who Must File、Exceptions、Foreign-owned U.S. DEs、Part V和Penalties。无须报告交易等例外须依原文判断；没有营业收入并不自动排除出资/分配类交易。2026-10-08另查About Form 5472，Recent developments显示None at this time。
