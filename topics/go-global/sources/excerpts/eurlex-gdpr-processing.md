# GDPR — processing contract and transfers

Read: 2026-10-10. EUR-Lex consolidated Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Article 28 and Chapter V. Applies subject to the Regulation's scope and the actual processing roles.

Article 28(3):

> Processing by a processor shall be governed by a contract or other legal act

Precise locators: 28(3) opening specifies subject matter, duration, nature/purpose, data types, data-subject categories and controller obligations/rights; (a) documented instructions; (b) confidentiality; (c) Article 32 measures; (d) other processors; (e)–(f) assistance; (g) return/delete and legal retention exception; (h) information and audits. Article 28(2) and (4) address other processors; 28(9) requires writing including electronic form. Article 44 separately subjects third-country transfers and onward transfers to Chapter V conditions. The chapter does not conclude a particular transfer mechanism or legal role for a real customer.
