# Payouts Process

- Source: https://docs.dodopayments.com/features/payouts/payout-structure
- Publisher: Dodo Payments
- Accessed: 2026-10-08

> Minimum Payout Threshold — Currency: USD, Default Threshold: $100, Lowest Threshold You Can Set: $50
> 
> If your payable balance does not meet the threshold, it carries forward to the next payout cycle.

> A payout with the status Success has been sent to your bank.

> Payout routes and currencies vary by country. Contact support@dodopayments.com to confirm how your payouts are sent and credited before you link a bank account.

本轮补核：默认半月周期，上半月在当月18日、下半月在次月4日发起；周末及美国联储银行假日顺延。可用金额、审核和出款周期共同决定是否发起。银行及中转行收费可能减少实收额。
