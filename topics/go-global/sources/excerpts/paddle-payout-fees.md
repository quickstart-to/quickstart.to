# Is there a fee taken for payouts?

- Source: https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/get-paid/is-there-a-fee-taken-for-payouts
- Publisher: Paddle
- Accessed: 2026-10-09

> If the payout currency is different from your bank's country ... the transfer must be sent internationally via SWIFT. These transfers will incur a wire fee of $/€/£15.

## 2026-10-09 提现章复核

定位：Wire Transfer 段。适用的 15 USD/EUR/GBP 电汇费在 invoice 创建前已扣除，invoice amount 是 amount sent。当前余额也已扣 Paddle fees；中转行及接收机构费用另外发生。非余额币种 conversion margin 最多 1.5%。避免从净额重复减费。
