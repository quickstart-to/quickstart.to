# Quickstart

- Source: https://docs.creem.io/getting-started/quickstart
- Publisher: Creem
- Accessed: 2026-10-08

> Go to the products tab in your dashboard and create your first product with a name, description, and price.

> Click the Share button on your product to get your payment link.

> For production apps, use Webhooks to reliably receive payment events on your server.
