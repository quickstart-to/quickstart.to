# Account Verification

- Source: https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/verification-process
- Publisher: Dodo Payments
- Accessed: 2026-10-08

> Individual	Product Information → Identity Verification (KYC) → Bank Verification

> We use Persona, a global verification provider, to verify your government-issued ID and confirm liveness with a selfie.

> For individuals, the account holder’s name must match your Identity Verification document.

> Live payments and payouts turn on separately. It’s normal to accept live payments while payouts are still inactive.

> Payouts require:
> Every required form approved, including Bank Verification
> Monitoring Review completed
