# Recovering your account if you lose your 2FA credentials

- 来源：https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/securing-your-account-with-two-factor-authentication-2fa/recovering-your-account-if-you-lose-your-2fa-credentials
- 发布方：GitHub Docs；实读：2026-10-09；置信度：confirmed，仅所述规则

> If you cannot use any recovery methods, you have permanently lost access to your account.

定位：开头警告、Using a two-factor authentication recovery code、Authenticating with a passkey/security key，以及没有恢复码时的 recovery-factor 流程。邮箱验证本身不等于恢复完成；要有支持的其他可用因素。SSH/PAT/已验证设备等按具体资格审核，不能承诺客服必定解锁。页面几处等待时间措辞不同，本章不采用固定时长。
