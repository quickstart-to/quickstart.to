# About campaign total budgets

- URL: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486938?hl=en
- Accessed: 2026-10-10
- 定位：Difference between total budgets and average daily budgets；How to use campaign total budgets

> There are no daily spending limits, and you will never be charged more than your total budget.

支持范围：总预算适用于新建 Search 等系列，需起止日期；现有日预算系列不能改预算类型；无每日消费上限，总计收费不超过系列总预算，Search 可用 Maximize clicks 等策略。
