Levels 3–5 describe defining a trial goal, involving relevant evaluators, checking purchasing boundaries and approval authority, and identifying who signs. The later stakeholder section separates procurement, security, legal and financial interests. These support questions to ask the buyer, not a fixed count of people or universal timeline. Do not adopt the article's suggestion to provide a fake phone number or its caricatures as general facts.
